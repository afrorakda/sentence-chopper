# #96 Sentence Chopper | Fridge Combo

A powerful writing-aid tool designed for global professionals. It helps you "kill your darlings" by physically breaking down long, complex English sentences into punchy, clear statements. Write less, say more.

## 核心 (Core Philosophy)
* **1 Tool / 1 Action**: Paste your text and witness the structural "weight" of your sentences.
* **Visual Awareness**: Highlights the "thorn" of long-winded writing by using forced line breaks at connectors.
* **Global Standard**: Built for the global business mantra: "One sentence, one idea."

## 機能 (Features)
* **Visual Chop Mode**: Automatically breaks lines at connectors like `and`, `but`, `because`, and `which`. See exactly where your sentences become too long.
* **Intelligent Finalizer**: 
    * Removes unnecessary connectors from the start of lines.
    * Automatically capitalizes the first letter of each new sentence.
    * Ensures each sentence ends with a proper period `.`.
* **Smart Spacing Toggle**: Tap "Finalize" repeatedly to toggle between Single Spacing (`\n`) and Double Spacing (`\n\n`) for different platforms (Email vs. WhatsApp).
* **Alert System**: Flashes a red warning and displays a badge if a single sentence exceeds 20 words.
* **Zero Shift UI**: Floating toast notifications appear at the very top of the screen, ensuring zero layout shift and no finger-blocking during operation.

## デザイン要件 (Design System)
* **Background**: #fff
* **Main Color**: #333
* **Accent**: #eee / #fafafa (input)
* **Border Radius**: 15px (Consistent with all Fridge Combo tools)
* **Feedback**: Tactile button responses and visual border-flashing for long sentences.
